Account Executive, Mid-Market (B2B SaaS)
Vantora Systems | Mumbai, India (Hybrid) | Full-time

About Vantora
Vantora sells workflow automation software to finance and operations teams at mid-sized and large companies. We have about 600 customers and are growing the India and Middle East business. Our sales team is 45 people across SDRs, Account Executives, Sales Engineers and Sales Operations.

About the role
As a Mid-Market Account Executive you will own the full sales cycle, from the first conversation to a signed contract. You will build your own pipeline alongside our SDR team, qualify opportunities against our MEDDICC framework, and give leadership an accurate view of your quarter. Typical deals are 40,000 to 300,000 USD in annual contract value with sales cycles of two to five months and several stakeholders, including finance, IT and procurement.

What you will do
- Generate pipeline through outbound prospecting (email, phone, LinkedIn and referrals) as well as follow-up on inbound leads.
- Run discovery conversations and qualify every opportunity using MEDDICC: metrics, economic buyer, decision criteria, decision process, identified pain, champion and competition.
- Manage a pipeline of 25 to 40 open opportunities, keep deals moving, and be clear about next steps and risks.
- Deliver tailored demos with a Sales Engineer and build a business case that shows return on investment.
- Lead commercial and contract negotiation, including pricing, terms and procurement and legal reviews.
- Keep Salesforce up to date so opportunity stages, close dates and amounts can be trusted.
- Provide a weekly and monthly sales forecast to your manager and defend the numbers in forecast calls.
- Work with Marketing, Customer Success and Sales Operations on account plans and handoffs.

Required qualifications
- 3 to 6 years of quota-carrying B2B software sales experience, including full-cycle selling.
- A track record of meeting or exceeding quota (for example, 600,000 to 1 million USD in annual new business).
- Salesforce: proficiency in managing opportunities, reports and dashboards.
- Outbound prospecting: experience generating your own pipeline, not only working inbound leads.
- Pipeline management: able to manage a multi-stage pipeline with discipline.
- MEDDICC (or MEDDPICC): working knowledge, and the habit of using it to qualify deals.
- Contract negotiation: experience leading pricing and terms discussions with procurement or legal.
- Sales forecasting: experience producing a forecast and being accountable for its accuracy.
- Excellent written and verbal communication.

Nice to have
- Experience selling to finance or operations buyers.
- Familiarity with Gong, Outreach, Salesloft or LinkedIn Sales Navigator.
- Experience selling in the Middle East or Southeast Asia.
- A bachelor's degree.

What we offer
Base salary plus uncapped commission, a clear path to Enterprise AE, structured onboarding, and a team that shares what works.

Vantora is an equal opportunity employer. We evaluate candidates on skills and results.
